## Honest exceptions to a "clean sweep" (carried forward from Phase 17, not new)

This checklist is complete, but completeness is not the same as "everything worked." The paper
must continue to state, wherever these results are cited: hybrid-vs-dense is not statistically
significant (p=0.146); OOD rejection improvement is not statistically confirmed at n=15 (p=0.25);
ambiguity detection is weak (F1=0.31); functional evaluation covers only 10% of the benchmark by
design. These are not blockers to freezing the project — they are the honestly-scoped boundaries
of what this specific research program established, which is exactly what a freeze checklist is
supposed to lock in.

## What happens after this checklist

Per the plan: **no further implementation should occur.** Any future work belongs to the
"Future Work" section of the manuscript (Section 19) — a larger benchmark, a richer ambiguity
feature, extended functional-evaluation coverage, an optional local-LLM comparator, a
human-preference study — not to further edits of the frozen `research/improvement` branch.
