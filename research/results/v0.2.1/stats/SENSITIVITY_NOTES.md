# Bare-keyword sensitivity analysis (post hoc, exploratory) — 2026-09-26

Script: `research/experiments/run_sensitivity_bare_keyword.js` → `sensitivity-bare-keyword-results.json`.
Paper table generated by `research/experiments/generate_sensitivity_table.js`.

**Status: post hoc.** Run after the raw v0.1/v0.2 results and the Holm results were known, in response to
the blind re-annotation (κ=0.6316) and an external critique. Not confirmatory.

## Rule (declared before looking at any outcome)

A *bare-keyword* query is an AMBIGUOUS-labeled query with exactly one whitespace-delimited token.
Applied uniformly: v0.1 has 9 (git, docker, ssh, network, permissions, process, kubernetes, files, curl),
the 17 new v0.2 ambiguous queries TA-B186–TA-B202 are all bare-keyword, total 26 of 38 ambiguous.
The rule is only a proxy: of the 3 items the reviewer disputed, two are single-token (ffmpeg, pip) and one
("list running processes") is not. The `ANSWERABLE` subset (drops all 38 ambiguous) is the label-uncertainty-free one.

## Reproduction guard

The script aborts unless the ALL subset reproduces the already-reported numbers (v0.1/v0.2 exact-McNemar p,
v0.1/v0.2 ECE before/after). All 7 checks passed.

## What was and was not re-run

- Re-run: accuracy comparisons (exact McNemar + 10k paired bootstrap, seed 42), calibration (isotonic re-fit on kept ids,
  same fold assignment, one-sided bootstrap p), ambiguity-detection AUROC (margin feature, mean of per-fold AUROC).
- NOT re-tuned: fusion weight α and detector thresholds (tuned once on the full benchmark).
- NOT recomputed: OOD rejection. Relabeling ambiguous↔answerable cannot change it (both are non-OOD negatives) and the
  OOD query set is identical in every subset.

## Findings

1. **The v0.2 "non-replication" of BM25→hybrid rests on one query.** The only query where BM25 succeeds and hybrid
   fails is TA-B194 ("system"), a bare keyword the reviewer *agreed* is ambiguous (RV-08 → B), so it is not a label dispute.
   Without it the comparison is 7–0 (p=0.0156), identical to v0.1. The gain is stable in size (+4.9 to +5.8 on every
   reduced subset) but its significance is decided by one query out of 8 discordant.
2. **Hybrid-over-dense on v0.2 (Holm p=0.025) depends on three new bare-keyword queries** (jq, grep, shell):
   3–14 → 3–11, p 0.013 → 0.057 (unadjusted), and 0.227 on answerable-only.
3. **Calibration is robust**: 77–84% relative ECE reduction in every row, one-sided p ≤ 0.0001.
4. **v0.2 adds no answerable evidence**: the 121 answerable queries are identical in v0.1 and v0.2. This supports
   the critique that v0.2 is an expanded, not independent, benchmark.
5. Ambiguity AUROC (margin): v0.2 all 0.723 (reproduces the reported value); without the 17 new bare-kw 0.743; without
   all 26, 0.679 (12 positives). NaN on answerable-only (no positives). Coarse; F1 was not recomputed (needs nested re-tuning).

## Caveats

Post hoc; α/thresholds not re-tuned; subsets are small (n=121–159, 7–8 discordant pairs); the bare-keyword rule is a
proxy. Findings 1–2 are descriptive statements about which queries drive the tests, not new significance claims.
